Elderglow Ref Picker

Every reference made for the Elderglow asset swap, in two looks: Elderglow (low-poly, flat colours, like your current kit) and Pawty (glossy clay toy). Tap every image you like for each asset, as many as you want. Add a comment where you want changes. Then put your name in the bar at the bottom and press Submit picks: it downloads a small .md file with your picks and comments. Send that file to Ruben. Your picks stay in this browser, so you can come back and change them.
